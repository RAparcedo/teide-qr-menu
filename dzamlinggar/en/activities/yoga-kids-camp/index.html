Yoga Kids Camp

Yoga Kids Summer Camp: Yoga & Art — four weeks of conscious movement (Yantra Yoga and Kumar Kumari for children), breath-awareness exercises, relaxation and mindfulness, art workshops (drawing, painting, ceramics) and nature-based collaborative games and storytelling, for children aged 5 to 12, in the gardens of Dzamling Gar, Playa Paraíso.

Classes are bilingual, in Spanish and English. Children bring their own snacks from home; lunch can be added separately, with vegetarian, vegan and allergy-friendly options.

Dates
6 July – 31 July 2026
Sessions
Monday to Friday, 9:00–14:00, with an extended stay until 15:00 available.
Teacher
Nataly Nitsche & Fabiana Esca

About the teacher

Nataly Nitsche & Fabiana Esca

Nataly Nitsche is the creator of the project, trained as an inclusive-education teacher and passionate about the movement arts. Fabiana Esca is an educator and art therapist with a degree in Conservation of Cultural Heritage.

Registration

Four weeks of yoga, art and nature for children aged 5 to 12, in the gardens of Dzamling Gar, Playa Paraíso.
