Activities

What is on at the Gar

Practice runs at Dzamling Gar all year: a weekly schedule of sessions at the Gar, and the courses, retreats and events that visiting teachers bring through the season.

Some practices are open to everyone; others are reserved for those who have already received teachings from Chögyal Namkhai Norbu or Namkhai Yeshi. Each event says which it is.

  • For practitioners

The weekly schedule

The weekly practices change from week to week, so we do not print a fixed timetable here. These two pages carry the current schedule — and if you are not sure which one is for you, or you would like the schedule for the week you are visiting, just write to us and we will send it.

Courses, retreats and events

Everything coming up at the Gar, in the order it happens. Some sessions can also be followed online.

July 2026

11 July – 12 September 2026

On site & online

For practitioners

Dorje Sempa Namkha Che — Through the Lens of Tibetan Language

Teacher Fabian Sanders

A study course exploring "Dorje Sempa Namkha Che" by examining the Tibetan-language words and the interpretations behind them in depth, beyond simple translation.

Requires transmission from Chögyal Namkhai Norbu or Namkhai Yeshi

18 July – 24 July 2026

On site

For practitioners

The Dance of the Song of the Vajra — Part 2

Teacher Ludmila Kislichenko

The Dance of the Vajra relates to the syllables of the Song of the Vajra: participants sing while they dance, following the correspondence between the sounds and the physical body.

Requires transmission from Chögyal Namkhai Norbu or Namkhai Yeshi

Register — The Dance of the Song of the Vajra — Part 2 →

August 2026

7 August – 9 August 2026

On site & online

For practitioners

Integration With the Elements & the Practice of Contemplation

Teacher Julia Lawless

A weekend exploring the Dzogchen elemental practices — Zernga, Semdzin, Rushen, Mantra and the Natural Integration methods of the Drathal Gyur Tantra.

Requires transmission from Chögyal Namkhai Norbu or Namkhai Yeshi

Register — Integration With the Elements & the Practice of Contemplation →

14 August – 16 August 2026

On site & online

For practitioners

Green Tara & Nature: Enlightenment Embodied

Teacher Julia Lawless

A weekend exploring the practice of Green Tara across its outer, inner and secret levels: her role as the Great Mother who governs the natural world, her embodiment of authentic compassion, and her indivisibility from the pristine nature of the mind.

Requires transmission from Chögyal Namkhai Norbu or Namkhai Yeshi
