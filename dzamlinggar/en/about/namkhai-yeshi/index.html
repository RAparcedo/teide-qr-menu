Spiritual guide of the International Dzogchen Community

Namkhai Yeshi

Born 1970

Son and student of Chögyal Namkhai Norbu. Recognized as a child as the reincarnation of Jamyang Khyentse Chökyi Wangchuk, he guides the Community his father founded.

Biography

Namkhai Yeshi is the son and student of Chögyal Namkhai Norbu (1938–2018), the founder of Merigar. He was born in Italy in 1970 and grew up with both a Christian and a Buddhist education.

As a child he was recognized by H.H. Sakya Trizin as the reincarnation of his maternal uncle, Jamyang Khyentse Chökyi Wangchuk (1909–1960), and was given the name Jamyang Chökyi Nyima. In 2007 he travelled to Tibet and visited the monastery of his predecessor.

He studied filmmaking, philosophy and computer science, and worked for years in information technology for mid-sized companies. He also trained for five years in electronic music at the Luigi Cherubini Conservatory in Florence, and composes electroacoustic music as a way of making ancient knowledge and the experience of meditation accessible.

At Dzamling Gar he leads the Gönpa Hall Acoustic Treatment Project, improving the sound quality of the community's main hall.

Leads this project

Gönpa Hall Acoustic Treatment Project

€43,000 Fundraising goal

Started in the summer of 2024 to improve the sound quality of the Gönpa Hall. An acoustic assessment was carried out, and the work now consists of installing sound-absorbing elements and acquiring the necessary equipment.

The mobile wall structures are complete and the acoustic materials have arrived; the curtains are being prepared. We aim to finish before Namkhai Yeshi's retreat in February 2026.

The lineage

The teachings practised here were transmitted by Chögyal Namkhai Norbu, who founded the International Dzogchen Community and identified this location in Tenerife as an ideal place for their diffusion and practice. They continue today with his son, Namkhai Yeshi, the holder of his transmission and lineage.
