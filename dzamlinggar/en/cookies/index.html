Cookie Policy

Last updated
14 July 2026
Version
1.0.0

1. The short version

This website sets no cookies of its own. It remembers one thing, and only in your own browser: the choice you made in the cookie banner. Everything else — the map on the Contact page — is only loaded once you say yes, and nothing at all is requested from a third party before that.

Accepting and rejecting are equally easy: the banner gives both, side by side, and you can change your mind at any time through Manage cookie preferences at the bottom of every page.

2. The four categories

We use the four categories of the AEPD’s 2024 guidance.

Strictly necessary (técnicas) — always on

What the site needs in order to work at all. There is exactly one entry, and it is not even a cookie: it is a record kept in your browser’s local storage.

Preferences (preferencias) — off until you allow it

Analytics (análisis) — off, and empty

This website measures nothing. There is no analytics tool of any kind, no visitor counter and no heat map. The category exists in the banner so that it can be honestly reported as switched off, and so that we cannot switch one on later without asking you.

Behavioural advertising (publicidad comportamental) — off, and empty

Never used. Dzamling Gar does not advertise on this website and does not let anybody track you across other websites from it.

3. Changing your mind

Open Manage cookie preferences at the bottom of any page. You can switch a category off again, or withdraw everything at once — the map is taken back off the page the moment you do.

4. Being asked again

We ask you again when this policy’s version number changes (it is 1.0.0 today), and in any case at most every 24 months, as the AEPD requires for the analytics and advertising categories.
