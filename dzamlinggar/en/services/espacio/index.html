Espacio Yoga Studio

Achieve well-being at ESPACIO Yoga Studio

We are dedicated to the study and practice of Yantra Yoga (Tibetan Yoga), breathing, dance and meditation — to nourish body, energy and mind.

The studio sits among the gardens of Dzamling Gar, at Playa Paraíso.

What we practise

  • 01 Yantra Yoga (Tibetan Yoga)
  • 02 Breathing
  • 03 Dance
  • 04 Meditation
  • 05 Yantra Yoga for children
  • 06 Wellness events

The studio

110 m² Area

An underground studio with padded flooring, mats, cushions and large mirrors. Toilets, showers and step-free access.

Class times

Our classes change with the season, so we do not print a fixed timetable here. Write to us for the classes running this week — or open the schedule of the Gar, which is always up to date.

How to find us

From Avenida Playa Paraíso, take the stone stairs up, follow the path on the right, and then the ramp on the left-hand side. The studio is underground, and there is step-free access.
