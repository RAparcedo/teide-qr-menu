Venue hire

Event and retreat spaces at Dzamling Gar

Discover versatile indoor and outdoor venues in a stunning, serene setting near Tenerife's coast.

Just a short walk from pristine beaches and the crystal-clear waters of the Atlantic, our location offers a serene backdrop for any occasion.

The spaces

Indoors and out: a hall above the sea, an underground gallery, a yoga studio, and ten thousand square metres of garden.

  1. 01

    900 m² Area

    up to 1,000 Capacity

    Conference Hall

    Wooden floors and ceilings, panoramic sea views and terraces facing La Gomera. Wood-framed glass doors on three sides, a small kitchen and bathrooms. Suited to conferences, weddings and large gatherings.

  2. 02

    Gallery Room (Underground Hall)

    Two sections — 300 m² with a sawdust-board floor and 500 m² with a concrete floor — plus toilets. A flexible layout for meetings and exhibitions.

  3. 03

    110 m² Area

    Espacio Yoga Studio

    An underground studio with padded flooring, mats, cushions and large mirrors. Toilets, showers and step-free access.

  4. 04

    10,000 m² Area

    Central Garden

    More than 500 plant species across five grass garden areas. Used for weddings, yoga, wellbeing and meditation, and for botanical and nature events.

Architecture

Benedetta Tagliabue

Designed by the architect Benedetta Tagliabue, each space seamlessly blends contemporary style with functional elegance.
