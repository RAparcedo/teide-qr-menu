Services

Spaces, yoga, wellness and stays

Dzamling Gar offers versatile event spaces, yoga classes, wellness retreats, and experiential stays in a serene coastal setting in Tenerife.

We are delighted to offer the following services to the public and to members of the International Dzogchen Community.

Here at the Gar

These two have their home at Dzamling Gar itself — the spaces we open for events and retreats, and the yoga studio among the gardens.

Event and Retreat Spaces

  • Conference Hall · 900 m²
  • up to 1,000 people

Discover versatile indoor and outdoor venues in a stunning, serene setting near Tenerife's coast. Designed by the architect Benedetta Tagliabue, each space combines elegance and functionality — for conferences, weddings, concerts, photo sessions, wellness retreats, festivals and corporate events.

On request

Espacio Yoga Studio

  • Espacio Yoga Studio · 110 m²

Dedicated to the study and practice of Yantra Yoga (Tibetan Yoga), breathing, dance and meditation to nourish body, energy and mind — including Yantra Yoga for children and wellness events, among the gardens of Playa Paraíso.

On request
