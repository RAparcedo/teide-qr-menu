About Dzamling Gar

A retreat and cultural center in Tenerife

Dzamling Gar is a retreat and cultural center located on the beautiful island of Tenerife in the Canary Islands. With a privileged climate and natural environment, the location was identified by Dzogchen Master Chögyal Namkhai Norbu as an ideal place for the diffusion and practice of the precious Dzogchen teachings.

Dzamling Gar is a place to develop presence and awareness for the wellbeing of one's self and all sentient beings.

The Dance of the Song of the Vajra — Part 2

18 July – 24 July 2026 · On site · Teacher: Ludmila Kislichenko

The Dance of the Vajra relates to the syllables of the Song of the Vajra: participants sing while they dance, following the correspondence between the sounds and the physical body. The course teaches the steps on the Mandala connected to their syllables, and the coordination of movement with time. The teaching belongs to Chögyal Namkhai Norbu's Longsal series. This is Part 2, taught at Dzamling Gar by Ludmila Kislichenko.

Requires transmission from Chögyal Namkhai Norbu or Namkhai Yeshi

Gönpa Hall Acoustic Treatment Project

€43,000 Fundraising goal

Started in the summer of 2024 to improve the sound quality of the Gönpa Hall. An acoustic assessment was carried out, and the work now consists of installing sound-absorbing elements and acquiring the necessary equipment.

The mobile wall structures are complete and the acoustic materials have arrived; the curtains are being prepared. We aim to finish before Namkhai Yeshi's retreat in February 2026.

Project lead: Namkhai Yeshi
