Summer camp · 3rd edition

A Magical Summer at Yoga Kids Camp: Yoga & Art!

July 6th to July 31st 2026 (Monday to Friday)

Four weeks of yoga, art and nature for children aged 5 to 12, in the gardens of Dzamling Gar, Playa Paraíso.

  • Ages 5–12
  • Classes are given in Spanish and English.
  • On site, at Dzamling Gar

The programme

  1. 01 Conscious movement — Yantra Yoga and Kumar Kumari for children
  2. 02 Breath awareness through sound
  3. 03 Relaxation and mindfulness
  4. 04 Art workshops — drawing, painting, ceramics and nature-inspired techniques
  5. 05 Games and storytelling
  6. 06 Nature exploration in the gardens

Classes are given in Spanish and English.

A day at the camp

The camp runs from 9:00 to 14:00 every day, and can be extended to 15:00 for €25 per week or €6 per day. Children bring their own snacks from home. Lunch can be added for €35 per week or €7.50 per day (please pre-register).
