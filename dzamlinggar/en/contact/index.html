Come and visit us

Dzamling Gar is in Playa Paraíso, Adeje, in the south of Tenerife. Write to us, send a WhatsApp message, or simply come to the InfoPoint during opening hours.

Come and see us

The gardens and the InfoPoint are open every day of the week. If you are coming as a group, tell us at least one day beforehand so that someone can receive you properly.

Finding us on foot

From Avenida Playa Paraíso, take the stone stairs up and follow the path on the right; the ramp on the left-hand side brings you into the Gar.

Dzamling Gar

Dzamling Gar is a nonprofit organization dedicated to the evolution of the human being. We are a member of the International Dzogchen Community founded by Tibetan Master Chögyal Namkhai Norbu.
